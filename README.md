Experiment 1

Aim To design and verify Half Adder, Full Adder and 4-bit Adder using Logisim.

Theory A Half Adder adds two bits and produces Sum and Carry. A Full Adder adds three bits (including carry input). A 4-bit adder is formed by cascading full adders.

Procedure Open Logisim Design Half Adder using XOR and AND gates Design Full Adder using logic gates Connect 4 Full Adders to form 4-bit adder Apply inputs and verify outputs

Result The Half Adder, Full Adder and 4-bit Adder were designed and verified successfully.
